# Primary source discovery / citation policy

## Imported primary source

United States v. Eichman, 496 U.S. 310 (1990), No. 89-1433:
[official Library of Congress PDF](https://tile.loc.gov/storage-services/service/ll/usrep/usrep496/usrep496310/usrep496310.pdf).
Provenance, rights rationale, case scope, exact page mappings and known limits are
recorded in `FIRST_CASE.md` and `src/judge_dredd/data/eichman/case.json`.
The accompanying criminal proceeding is not assumed closed merely because it
appears in the same opinion (p. 313 n. 1).

## Discovery repositories

Potential repositories (links are starting points, not verified selections of cases):
- ICC: https://www.icc-cpi.int/
- IRMCT / ICTY case records: https://www.irmct.org/
- European Court of Human Rights HUDOC: https://hudoc.echr.coe.int/
- England and Wales Sentencing Council: https://sentencingcouncil.org.uk/
- US Sentencing Commission: https://www.ussc.gov/research/datafiles/commission-datafiles
- World Values Survey: https://www.worldvaluessurvey.org/
- World Justice Project: https://worldjusticeproject.org/rule-of-law-index
- Ukraine Unified State Register of Court Decisions: https://reyestr.court.gov.ua/

For each acquired document preserve URL, court, docket number, document version, date, retrieval time, license/publication status, file SHA-256, pages/paragraphs, translation provenance, and appeal status.

**Do not assume unrestricted bulk-scraping rights or stable APIs.** Review source terms and privacy restrictions before bulk ingestion.

**Citation rule:** factual claims require a pinpoint source; a verified document can contain allegations. Cite the particular finding or sentence, not just the website homepage.
