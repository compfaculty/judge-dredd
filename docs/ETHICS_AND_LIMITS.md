# Operational boundaries and limitations

Judge Dredd is a speculative name for an **explainable research tool**, not an authority to arrest, punish or carry out lethal force.

1. No autonomous coercion, execution, imprisonment, fines, retaliation, or targeting of persons.
2. Evidence-based individualized assessment. Group identity, nationality and obligatory tax payment alone are not crimes.
3. Separate state-level reparations from individual liability; good deeds are tracked independently of past harm.
4. Legal elements and jurisdiction-specific standards are mandatory for legal analysis; historical moral judgment must not retroactively invent criminal laws.
5. Rights constraints cannot be bypassed by expected-gain optimization.
6. Any risk estimate needs domain validation, dataset-shift evaluation and human oversight.
7. Disagreement with public opinion is not proof of moral superiority; public opinion is descriptive evidence, not the system's authority.
8. All learning changes are staged, benchmarked, audited and reversible.
9. Public reports should disclose model limitations, source gaps and reviewer roles.
