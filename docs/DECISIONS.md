# Design decisions from the discussion

- Rename project Global Moral Atlas → **Judge Dredd** (Global Moral Atlas retained as descriptive research subsystem).
- Keep distinct: objective/observed harm, personal causal contribution, legal guilt, normative severity, future harm, uncertainty, reparations and positive actions.
- Treat current evidence and today's applicable review as primary for operational cases; store historical legal and moral context without retrospective criminalization.
- No automatic court verdict. Escalate moral conflict / insufficient evidence / serious consequences to human review, and preserve roles of system and human.
- Reviewer feedback helps evaluate candidates but does not rewrite normative core or automatically certify correctness.
- Accept irreducible errors; minimize calibrated errors and resulting harm, and explicitly abstain when confidence is unsupported.
- Moral Invariant Core protects rights and individual responsibility from majority drift; revise only through formal governance, not polling.
- Do not let beneficial state-building outcomes cancel out violations against individuals automatically.
- First validate actual legal-document extraction and provenance; only later develop numeric MSU, dynamical and cross-cultural models.
- All math requires formal and empirical audit; no formulas certified by AI assertion.
