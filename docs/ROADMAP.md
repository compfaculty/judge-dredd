# Delivery plan and backlog

## Milestone 0 — repository scaffold (included)
- App and database models, synthetic seeded demo, minimal evidence inventory API, human review endpoint, experimental math and tests, concept docs.

## Milestone 1 — first genuine case
- Implemented first curated import: `python -m judge_dredd.import_case`; see `FIRST_CASE.md`. Official PDF retained, hash checked, 15 pages extracted, scoped procedural history, assertions and element coverage stored; assessment frozen and human review linked/audited.
- Remaining: independent legal/source review, broader trial materials, migrations and a genuinely blinded evaluation. The current assessment is explicitly outcome-exposed.
- Official primary documents and license/retention checks.
- PDF extraction with page anchors and source hashes.
- Structured allegations vs court findings; timeline, charges, procedural history, provenance.
- Saved initial assessment before showing actual verdict, when feasible.

## Milestone 2 — usable local UI
- HTMX case list, source pane, quote/citation explorer, charge-element matrix, jury/researcher verdict panel.
- Assessment versions, independent feedback, reasoning diffs.

## Milestone 3 — trustworthy evidence engine
- OCR for scans only when necessary; human verification of extraction.
- Dependency-aware evidence confidence without invented probabilities.
- Chain of custody / tamper-evident event log, authentication/redaction and independent audit.

## Milestone 4 — math research
- BT preference experiment, multidimensional partial order, calibration, Bayesian uncertainty, sensitivity analysis, failure/abstain routes.
- No promotion of research formulas into binding sentencing decisions.

## Milestone 5 — historical and cross-cultural studies
- Law versions, historical legal context, representative moral survey samples, subgroup variation, drift monitoring, model review gates.

## Definition of done
- Tested, reproducible, linked sources, no hidden legal claims, metrics, model cards and documented failure modes.
